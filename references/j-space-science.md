## Research claims and their scope

The cited workspace research is described as studying verbalizable representations
with activation readouts and interventions, including report and multi-step tasks.
Those methods require model access and controls unavailable to an ordinary prompt.
A model's account of its own processing is not an activation measurement.

The cited introspection and emotion work concerns particular elicitation or intervention
conditions. Its reported effects cannot establish the reliability of ordinary suspicion,
prove that written markers reproduce injected directions, or verify subjective experience.

Compression, rereading, and metacognitive-control papers concern specific experimental
procedures and benchmarks. Their results do not establish that this complete skill helps.
Long-horizon evaluations describe agent failures and possible context-management remedies.
Their findings need to be checked against the tested models, budgets, and environments.

Research on human cognition supplies analogies, not validation for a language model.
Commentary and reported traces can suggest hypotheses but are weaker than controlled tests.
Unverified trace provenance and interpretations should not be presented as authenticated data.
No quantitative claim from the earlier digest is carried forward as a verified result.

## Operational hypotheses for this skill

These are design proposals to evaluate, rather than conclusions established by the citations:

- Compact task state may reduce lost constraints and duplicated work after interruption.
- Shared definitions may make inconsistent artifacts easier to detect and repair.
- Independent checks may expose errors that repeated self-review misses.
- Explicit evidence scope may reduce unsupported claims of completion.
- Diagnosed retries may recover more efficiently than repeating an unchanged approach.
- Optional structure may avoid overhead on short tasks.

There is no universal one- or two-idea capacity rule in these workflows.
A clean review, unchanged confidence, or ordinary prose is not evidence of dysfunction.
Behavioral improvement would support a workflow choice, not a consciousness claim.
Use the [evaluation playbook](induction-playbook.md) to test against simpler baselines.
The [examples](exemplars.md) illustrate intended behavior without claiming measured gains.

## How to use a citation

Open the primary source before relying on a specific claim.
Check authorship, version, methods, model, sample, controls, and the result's denominator.
Distinguish what was measured from the authors' interpretation and your own inference.
Record actual access and verification limits; never invent authentication or replication.
If a source is unavailable, qualify or omit the dependent claim.
The following links are preserved for that review, not endorsed as verified evidence.
